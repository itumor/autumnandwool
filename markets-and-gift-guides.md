## 5. Notes and caveats

- **Competition at Doma laukums:** The 2025 participant list includes Ieva knitwear, Pārdaugavas darbnīca, ArtEgoLace, Tines and Anita Šendere. Expect selection to favour Latvian production.
- **Fees:** No Riga organiser publishes stall prices openly except KuKu Tirgus (€50) and the €2/day municipal fee at Doma laukums. The others show prices only inside the booking flow.
- **Paperwork commonly asked for:** company registration number, VAT number if any, bank details, cash register or receipt book details (MyExpo), electricity needs, product photos and a stall visual (Doma laukums). Doma laukums also asks for an e-signature (eParaksts) or a signed paper contract.
- **Possible stockists** (outside this brief): LIVE RĪGA lists local-product shops including M50, RIIJA Shop, MūsMāja, JO concept store and Hobbywool.
