## 1. Summary

Where we are: 0 real direct orders, 64 visits in 30 days (2 from Google, 0 from Instagram/Pinterest), 0 of 82 SKUs
costed (so no break-even CAC), no capacity list, PayPal not connected, no delivery times shown, GA4 pageviews only,
`kpi-weekly.csv` empty, opening cash unknown. The click-trap is fixed (19 Oct), Stripe works, Merchant Center has
98 rows under review. This is a traffic and readiness problem first, not an ad-efficiency problem. Every department
plan reaches the same conclusion: the work is gate-clearing, and one person (the owner/founder) applies, sends, films,
times garments and packs.

Bets (max 3):
1. **Q4 2026: make the store buyable and decidable.** Cost the 22 visible SKUs, PayPal, delivery times, size info,
   variant photos, Merchant Center approved, one weekly KPI row. Paid spend stays EUR 0 unless every gate passes.
2. **Q4 2026 to Q2 2027: earn the first free traffic and prove 1-2 channels** (Merchant Center free listings, Pinterest,
   creator gifting, Etsy move-to-site) with attribution we can verify. Paid only on costed, capacity-GREEN SKUs at or
   below break-even CAC.
3. **2027: be ready for the second season.** Production capacity, yarn and cash planned by August 2027 so the autumn
   2027 build and Q4 2027 run on measured data, with hires triggered by load and contribution, not by calendar.
