## 5. Risks and owner decisions needed

### Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Owner and Kristine are the only film crew; 2 hours a week is a hard cap | Output below 3 videos a week | One combined shot list per session; batch; reuse TikTok versions and stills |
| Traffic stays low even with content | Zero orders is the expected outcome at this scale (`zero-sales-diagnosis-2026-10-18.md` s4) | Judge content on UTM sessions first; re-set order targets after 4 weeks of data |
| Photo backlog delayed | Merchant Center cannot approve variant rows; gift season passes | Turtleneck first (36 rows); phone flat lays on plain oat background are enough per owner pack A |
| Hand-off miss: posting without Production's list | Promoting something that can't ship | Calendar lists the list date; no list means no product posts |
| Claim drift ("handmade", "sustainable", slow-fashion hashtags) | Breaks brand-voice hard gate and legal risk | Checklist on every draft; fix the 6 existing drafts; use "knitted on manual machines and finished by hand in Riga" |
| Legacy accounts (`@mare.knitwear`, Mareshop Facebook and Pinterest) confuse the brand | Split audience, wrong tag | Owner confirms ownership; no posting to Facebook or Pinterest until then |
| Etsy quotes and customer photos used without consent | Trust and legal | Written consent first; verbatim, labelled "Etsy review" |
| Market applications need proof of Latvian production, brief doesn't state it | Wrong claim, or lost slot | Do not claim "made in Latvia"; owner confirms with organiser |
| AI-generated visuals taken as real | Misrepresents the garment | No AI product images; any AI moodboard labelled AI-CONCEPT and kept internal |
| Music and platform licensing | Takedown | Platform commercial library or original sound only |
