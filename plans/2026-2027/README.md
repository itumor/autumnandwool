# plans/2026-2027
Annual plan for Autumn & Wool, Q4 2026 and each quarter of 2027. All files are DRAFTS for owner approval. Nothing
is sent, published, bought or changed on any live account. Unknown values are `TODO` or `[VERIFY]`.
How produced: 2026-10-21, 10 subagents. 9 department agents ran in parallel (one plan each, same template:
starting facts, Q4 2026, 2027 by quarter, dependencies, risks and owner decisions). Then the GM agent read all 9
plus `business-os/README.md`, `hiring-plan.md` and the zero-sales diagnosis, and wrote the merge.
Start with `master-plan.md` (summary, KPIs, critical path, roadmap, hiring order, owner decision list, conflict rulings).
