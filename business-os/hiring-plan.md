# Hiring Plan

Core team of about 7–9 including the founder/production lead. These are **roles, not nine salaries**: the founder
may own product + production, finance is an outsourced accountant, and one growth marketer can run ads + creators
at first. Add specialists only when order volume justifies it.

## Hiring order

| Wave | When | Roles |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Days 1–30 | Head of E-commerce / GM, Growth Marketing Manager, E-commerce & CRO Specialist. Accountant (outsourced), EU consumer-law adviser (one-off) |
| 2 | Days 31–60 | Creative Content Lead, Customer Experience & CRM Specialist |
| 3 | When orders exceed one person's packing/production capacity | Product & Production Manager (if founder can't hold it), Operations / Fulfilment Coordinator |
| 4 | When creator tests show profitable orders | Partnerships & Influencer Specialist |
| Later | Meaningful order volume | SEO/Pinterest Specialist, Email & Retention Manager, Performance Ads Specialist, Marketplace Manager, Data Analyst, AI Automation Engineer (only after processes run well by hand) |

## Job descriptions

### Head of E-commerce / General Manager
Turns Autumn & Wool into a profitable business. Builds the quarterly commercial plan, sets department targets,
approves campaigns, runs hiring, delivers a weekly P&L review.
- **Must have:** grew a DTC fashion or accessories brand; owned a P&L; EU shipping/returns experience.
- **First 90 days:** 90-day growth plan, hiring roadmap, financial dashboard (`kpi-weekly.csv`), weekly
  scale/stop decisions, costs filled for all visible SKUs.

### Growth Marketing Manager
Defines audiences, researches competitors, runs paid ads, evaluates channels, controls acquisition economics.
- **Must have:** Meta + Google Ads hands-on, small-budget testing discipline, reads contribution not ROAS alone.
- **First 90 days:** weekly campaign tests capped at break-even CAC (`unit_economics.py`), landing-page briefs,
  creative performance report, plan to grow profitable orders.

### Creative Content Lead
Plans and produces real product imagery, model shoots, short video, founder stories, demos. Uses AI for concepts
and editing without misrepresenting garments.
- **Must have:** fashion/textile portfolio, phone + camera video, styling eye.
- **First 90 days:** variant photo per colour for every visible SKU; one weekly batch of reusable assets for social,
  ads and PDPs; 3–5 short videos/week.

### E-commerce & CRO Specialist
Owns the online shopping experience: product copy, sizing, payments, trust signals, speed, tracking.
- **Must have:** Squarespace (or Shopify) commerce, GA4, mobile QA, structured experiments.
- **First 90 days:** 5 priority PDPs perfected, PayPal live, monthly real test order, Merchant Center approved,
  weekly site improvements with before/after numbers.

### Product & Production Manager
Makes winning designs producible consistently, profitably and on time.
- **Must have:** knitwear/textile production, costing, supplier management.
- **First 90 days:** SKU cost sheets (`sku-economics.csv`), production schedule, reorder points, stock-risk report,
  QC checklist incl. EU fibre-composition labels, weekly capacity + safe-to-promote list.

### Operations / Fulfilment Coordinator
- **First 90 days:** order-to-delivery SOP (README section 6), carrier rates per region, returns log, dispatch
  within promised lead time.

### Customer Experience & CRM Specialist
- **First 90 days:** reply < 24 h, FAQ + size-advice macros (AI-drafted, human-sent), review request flow,
  MailerLite welcome + post-purchase emails.

### Partnerships & Influencer Specialist
- **First 90 days:** 10–20 small relevant creators contacted, gifting/affiliate terms, unique codes, report of
  orders and profit per creator.

## Interview test (all roles)

Give the candidate `README.md` section 2 and the illustrative order economics (section 9). Ask: "What are the first
three things you do in your area, and how will we know in 30 days if they worked?" Reject answers measured only in
views, followers or tasks done.
