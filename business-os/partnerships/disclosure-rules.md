# Creator disclosure rules + weekly check: DRAFT (PAR-08)

Status: DRAFT. Not legal advice. Summary in our own words of the EU Commission Influencer Legal Hub / Legal Brief 6
and PTAC guidance as reported by lvportals.lv (2024). **[VERIFY] the current PTAC text before relying on it**, and
add this page to the EU adviser questions (master decision 22) if the owner wants certainty.

## 1. The rules we follow (hard rule 5: never hide the relationship)

1. A product gifted so that it may be featured makes any resulting post **advertising**, even with no obligation
   to post and no money paid.
2. The label must be **clear and visible before people engage**: at the start of the caption, on the video or image,
   or spoken at the start. It must not sit only at the end of a hashtag list or in a "more" fold.
3. Use the platform's **paid-partnership / branded-content label** where it exists, **plus** a plain-language
   label.
4. Latvia (PTAC examples): `#reklama`, `#apmaksats`, `#sponsorets`. In EN: "Ad", "Advertisement", "Gifted by
   @autumnandwool" at the start.
5. **Not enough on their own:** "thanks @autumnandwool", "#collab", "#sp", "#partner", a tag in the image only.
6. Codes and affiliate links make it a commercial relationship too. Same label rules.
7. The creator's opinion must be genuine. We never script "honest" reviews, ask for positive-only posts, or pay
   for reviews.
8. A&W must not repost undisclosed creator content, or use it in ads, without the disclosure and written usage rights.

## 2. Weekly disclosure check (every posted item)

| Check | Pass if |
|---|---|
| Label present | Paid-partnership label and/or #reklama / "Ad" / "Gifted" |
| Visible early | First line of the caption, on screen in the first seconds, or the platform label |
| Language | Understood by the audience (LV for LV audiences) |
| No banned claims about A&W | No sustainable/eco/organic/hypoallergenic/itch-free/"made in Latvia"/"hand-knitted" |
| Code (if any) is correct | Matches the approved code and % |

Log in `creator-tracker.csv`: `posted_url`, `post_date`, `disclosure_ok` = `yes` / `no-fixed` / `no-open`.

## 3. Fix request (owner sends if a post fails)

```
Hi [first name], thank you so much for the post, it looks lovely. One small thing: because the piece was a gift,
EU and Latvian rules count it as an ad. Could you add "#reklama" or the paid-partnership label at the start
of the caption? Thank you, Kristine
```
For a banned claim:
```
Hi [first name], thank you for the post. One small correction: we can't describe our pieces as [claim] yet,
as we don't have the evidence on file. Could you remove that word? Thank you, Kristine
```
If it is not fixed within 7 days: do not reshare, close the creator in the tracker, and note the reason.

## 4. Other countries
Germany and the Nordics have their own rules and labels (for example, German practice often expects "Werbung" or
"Anzeige"). **[VERIFY] with an adviser before contacting any DE or Nordic creator.** Until then, Latvia only.
