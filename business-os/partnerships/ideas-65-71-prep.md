## 1. Idea 65: photos from past Etsy buyers (PAR-20)

What we found (2026-10-21, secondary sources, [VERIFY] in Etsy's own Seller Policy / Help Center before any step):
- Etsy guidance, as repeated on many shop privacy pages: under EU and other laws, marketing to a buyer needs their
  prior express consent. Having their email from an order is not consent. Unsolicited promotions through Etsy
  Messages break Etsy policy.
- EcommerceBytes (Jan 2024) reports Etsy told sellers not to copy buyer emails from orders into their own mailing
  lists without consent.
- Nothing found on reusing photos buyers posted in Etsy reviews. Treat them as the buyer's content: no reuse without
  written permission.

Proposed safe route (owner decides; CX's adviser list already proposes never importing Etsy buyers):
1. No bulk messages and no export of buyer emails.
2. Only where a buyer already posted a photo in a public review, the owner may ask once, through Etsy Messages in
   reply to an existing conversation, if Etsy's current policy allows it [VERIFY]. Not a promotion: a permission
   request only.
3. Written permission saved (screenshot), with scope: where (site, PDP, social), how long, credit or not.

Draft permission request (owner sends, only after O14 and the Etsy policy check):
```
Hi <first name>, thank you again for your review of the <product> on Etsy, and for the photo.
Would you allow Autumn & Wool (my own shop, autumnandwool.com) to show your photo on our product page
for <12 months>? We would credit you as "<first name>, Etsy buyer" or not at all, as you prefer.
No is completely fine. If yes, a short "yes, you may use it" here is enough.
Kristine
```
Open: CX consent wording (adviser), whether the adviser wants more than a message reply as proof.
