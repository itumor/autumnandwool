# CX questions for the adviser (CX-18)

Context: Autumn & Wool (AUTUMN AND WOOL, SIA, Riga) sells knitwear direct on Squarespace to Latvia, 18 EU
countries, US/CA/AU/UK/NO/CH. There are no real direct orders yet. Email platform planned: MailerLite (EU-based).
Drafts: `email-drafts/flow-plan.md`, `post-purchase.md`, `win-back.md`.

## A. Email consent
1. Newsletter form on the website: what must the form say, and is double opt-in advisable? What consent records
   (date, source, wording shown) should we keep, and for how long?
2. Direct buyers: may we send "similar products" emails under the existing-customer exception in Latvian law?
   If yes: what wording at checkout, what opt-out, and does it cover customers outside the EU?
3. Is a post-dispatch care email ("what to expect, how to wash it") a service message or marketing?
4. Is a fit-check email ("how does it fit?") or a review request marketing that needs consent?
5. Market stalls: is the paper card wording in `flow-plan.md` enough? How should we store the paper forms?
6. Former Etsy buyers: we propose never to import them. Please confirm there is no route we have missed.

## B. Reviews (Omnibus / unfair commercial practices)
7. Our review request asks every buyer, offers no incentive, and says "if something is not right, please reply
   to me first so I can help; you are free to review either way". Is that acceptable, or could it count as
   steering?
8. If reviews are shown on our site, what statement about how they are collected and checked is required?
9. May we show Etsy (Mareshop) reviews or the "4.9 stars, 1,400+ reviews on Etsy" line on our own site and in
   emails, if they are labelled as Etsy?

## C. Data requests
10. What should we do, and by when, when a customer asks for access to or deletion of their data (Squarespace
    orders, Gmail, MailerLite)? Which records must we keep anyway (e.g. invoices)?

## D. Returns replies (same issues as the OPS / Shipping & Returns questions)
11. Until the revised policy is published, may staff replies follow the statutory withdrawal and guarantee
    rules even where the live page is stricter (sale and made-to-order exclusions, shipping not refunded,
    5-day defect report)? What should replies say about this difference?
12. Gift purchases: who holds the withdrawal right when the recipient returns a gift, and how should a longer
    goodwill exchange window be worded so that it does not shorten statutory rights?
