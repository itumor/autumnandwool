## 3. Minimum contribution per order (owner sets 4 Nov, fallback 11 Nov; FIN proposes the number)
Status: **blocked on costs (G1) and fixed costs (G3).** No number proposed; any figure now would be invented.

Method once inputs exist:
1. Monthly fixed costs (owner list, decision 7) / realistic monthly orders = contribution each order must leave to
   cover overhead. With no order history, use orders the owner can make and pack per month (PRD capacity).
2. Cross-check against a floor as a share of net revenue (owner picks the %).
3. Max CAC per SKU = CM2 - minimum contribution. If max CAC is near zero, that SKU is organic-only.

Illustrative only (README s9 example, not real costs): EUR 155 sweater, CM2 EUR 33.10. If the minimum contribution
were EUR 25, max CAC would be EUR 8.10. A low ceiling like this is why the first two test channels above are free or
product-cost only.
