## Interview kit
### Squarespace commerce
- Behavioural: "A store bug that silently cost sales. How did you find it?" / "A fix you made without code access."
- Situational (A&W): "Beanie add-to-cart does nothing if no colour is chosen; we cannot add scripts. What do you do?"
### Measurement
- Behavioural: "When did you decide traffic was too low to A/B test? What did you do instead?"
- Situational: "64 visits/month, 94% bounce. Which 3 numbers do you report weekly?"
- Probes: before/after numbers; what you shipped personally.

## Work sample (under 2 h)
Audit one PDP screenshot (e.g. cardigan, sanitised): top 5 issues ranked by likely conversion impact, each with a
copy or layout diff. Scored on prioritisation and honesty about evidence.

## Debrief
