## A-GM-20 Minimum contribution per order: working figure (GM-05)

Method: `finance/min-contribution-method.md`. Min contribution = monthly fixed costs / orders per month, floored at a
fixed amount and rounded up to whole EUR. Every input is an assumption, so the figure is a **planning assumption**.
It is not a decision.

**Fixed-cost scenarios (A-GM-09, illustrative):** EUR 100 / 175 / 250 per month.
**Order-volume scenarios (illustrative; no history, 0 real orders):** 10 / 20 / 40 orders per month.

| Fixed costs EUR/mo | 10 orders/mo | 20 orders/mo | 40 orders/mo |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100 (Lean) | 10.00 | 5.00 | 2.50 |
| 175 (Mid) | 17.50 | 8.75 | 4.38 |
| 250 (High) | 25.00 | 12.50 | 6.25 |

**Assumed figure: EUR 10 per order** = Mid fixed costs at 20 orders/month (8.75), rounded up to the next EUR 5
step. Floor EUR 5 (no paid order may leave less than EUR 5 towards overheads). Sensitivity is run at EUR 5 / 10 / 15.
Why not higher: with 0 orders, a high minimum would rule out every paid test before any data exists. Why not lower:
below EUR 5 a paid order contributes nothing to overheads at any volume shown.

**Per-SKU check (Max CAC = CM2 - minimum):** `business-os/head-of-ecommerce/min-contribution-assumed-2026-10-21.md`
(CM2 by destination from A-PRD costs + A-OPS shipping/packaging).
