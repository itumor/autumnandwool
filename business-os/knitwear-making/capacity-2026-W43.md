## Market-stall production input (PRD-16), ASSUMPTION

stall stock, for example 40 beanies (1,400) + 6 hat sets (780) + 3 sweaters (630) = 2,810 min. Every stall day costs a full
production day (≈ 225 min). A multi-week market (Doma laukums 27 Nov – 3 Jan) would take most December capacity away from
online orders, so take one short market at most (agrees with GM ruling on decision 27). Stall stock is ring-fenced and not promotable online (PRD-24).
