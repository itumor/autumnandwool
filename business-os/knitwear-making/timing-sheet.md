## How to time (about 5 minutes of note-taking per unit)
1. Time 3 separate units of the same SKU, each step start to stop, minutes only. Exclude drying time (elapsed, not work).
2. Use the median of the 3 (middle value), not the average.
3. Weigh one finished garment (kitchen scale, grams). Note the yarn name and dye lot.
4. Note anything unusual (rework, interruptions) in the notes column. That unit can be re-timed.
