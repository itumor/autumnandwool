How to choose a wool cardigan

By Kristine, designer and maker at Autumn & Wool · Updated 10 November 2026

Choose a wool cardigan by style, length and fibre. Our open-front cardigans are straight-cut, the long shawl-collar cardigans have a draped collar, and the merino raglan cardigan has a relaxed shoulder line and comes in one size. Compare the garment measurements and the material line on each product page before you order.

Open front or shawl collar?

A shawl collar drapes softly at the neck and front. The open-front cardigan with side slits is straight-cut and has ribbed panels at the sides, so it has a simpler line. Both are 100% pure new wool, as their product pages state, so the choice comes down to the shape you prefer.

Is a raglan cardigan easy to wear?

A raglan sleeve runs from the underarm up to the neck, which gives a relaxed shoulder line. Our merino raglan cardigan is 100% merino wool and described as relaxed and slightly oversized. It comes in one size, with a listed width of 61 cm, so check the garment measurements on its product page before you choose.

How long should a cardigan be?

Length is the first thing to check. The long shawl-collar cardigans measure 83 to 86 cm in garment length from S to XL, and the open-front cardigan with side slits measures 70 cm. A longer cardigan covers more of the hip, so think about what you wear underneath. Use our size guide to compare your measurements with ours.

Which fibre and care suit a cardigan?

Pure new wool is wool that has not been reclaimed from old knitwear. Merino is finer than most wool, and neither fibre is automatically warmer. Warmth depends on the yarn and the knit, so compare the material line on each page. Hand wash your cardigan gently in cool water with a mild wool detergent, do not wring it, and lay it flat to dry.
