Oversized fit guide

By Kristine, designer and maker at Autumn & Wool · Updated 10 November 2026

Oversized and relaxed knits are cut wider and longer than close-fitting knitwear, so they sit away from the body. Choose a size by your body measurements from our size guide, then check the garment width and length on the product page. Oversized pieces are often one size, so compare the garment width with your chest.

What do relaxed and oversized mean?

Our size guide lists Close-fitting, Relaxed and Oversized fits, plus One size. Relaxed knits have room for layering. Oversized pieces are roomy and made for layering. Each product page gives its own fit note, such as 'Relaxed' or 'Oversized; roomy and easy to layer'. The Lightweight Merino Turtleneck is described as comfortable and close to the body, so check the fit before you choose.

How do you measure for an oversized sweater?

Measure your chest, waist and hips over thin, fitted clothing, with the tape snug but comfortable. Match your numbers to the body size chart below. Sizes S to XL convert to EU 36 to 42, UK 8 to 14 and US 6 to 12. For example, size S is 84 to 90 cm around the chest, 62 to 70 cm at the waist and 90 to 96 cm at the hips. Size M is 90 to 96 cm at the chest.

How do you compare the garment measurements?

Body measurements are only part of the picture. Oversized pieces are cut wider than the body, so compare the garment measurements on the product page with your chest. Double the garment width to get the full circumference, then compare it with your chest. The difference is the ease, the extra room in the piece. The size guide gives an example: a Pure Wool Side-Slit Sweater in size S measures 50 cm flat, which is about 100 cm (39.4 in) around.

For a close look at the numbers, see the Distressed Oversized Wool Raglan Sweater, which measures 57 cm wide, and the Oversized Merino Turtleneck, Dusty Pink, which measures 53 cm wide.

Which pieces come in one size?

Vests, some cardigans and oversized pieces are often one size. The size guide advises that you compare the garment width with your chest. Our sweater vests are often one size. The Merino Wool Raglan Cardigan comes in one size and is described as relaxed and slightly oversized. Other pieces list sizes from S to XL, or S/M and L/XL, so check the sizes on the product page before you order.

Does layering change the fit?

Oversized knits are made for layering, so think about what goes underneath. A sweater vest over a shirt gives a different shape from a loose knit over a fitted top. Our beige melange vest is described as easy to layer over shirts and dresses.
