How to wash a wool sweater

By Kristine, designer and maker at Autumn & Wool · Updated 10 November 2026

Wash a wool sweater by hand in cool water with a mild wool detergent. Do not use hot water, do not wring it, and do not tumble dry. Support the wet weight of the knit, lay it flat on a clean towel to dry, and keep it away from radiators and direct heat. Store it folded rather than hung.

What do you need before you start?

Check the care line on the product page first, because it is the source of truth. Our sweaters are merino wool, lambswool or pure new wool, depending on the style. Fill a basin with cool water and add a mild wool detergent. Do not use hot water, because heat and rubbing can make wool felt.

How do you hand wash a wool sweater?

Put the sweater in the water and press it gently so the water reaches the fibres. Keep the movement slow and light. Lift it out without wringing or twisting. Wringing stretches the knit and can distort the shape, so support the weight of the wet sweater with both hands.

Oversized styles follow the same steps. Because they have more fabric, support the whole piece while it is wet so it keeps its shape. Relaxed fits need the same gentle handling.

How do you dry a wool sweater?

Lay the sweater flat on a clean towel. Roll the towel gently to take out some water, then reshape the sweater by hand. Leave it to dry flat, away from radiators and direct heat. Do not tumble dry.

How do you store a wool sweater?

Store the sweater folded, not hung, because hanging can stretch the shoulders. Our FAQ suggests breathable bags with cedar or lavender to help keep moths away.

Do not iron over the knit. A small hole or a dropped stitch is easier to fix when it is caught early. If you are unsure what to do, ask us through the contact page before you wash the piece again.
