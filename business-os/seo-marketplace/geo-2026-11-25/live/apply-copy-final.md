### Who makes Autumn & Wool
Autumn & Wool is a small knitwear studio in Riga, Latvia. Kristine designs every piece. Panels are knitted on a manually operated knitting machine (machine knitting, not hand knitting), then hand-sewn, washed and pressed before shipping. We use merino wool, lambswool and pure new wool; the fibre is listed on each product page. Before Autumn & Wool, Kristine sold for about 15 years as Mareshop on Etsy. Size details are in the size guide; delivery and returns are on the shipping and returns page.

### Where is Autumn & Wool made?
In our studio in Riga, Latvia. Each piece is knitted on a manually operated knitting machine, hand-sewn, washed and pressed there.

### Is Autumn & Wool the same maker as Mareshop on Etsy?
Yes. Kristine sold knitwear for about 15 years as Mareshop on Etsy, and Autumn & Wool is her own shop. The Etsy reviews (4.9 from 1,400+ reviews) stay on Etsy.

### Is it hand-knitted?
No. Pieces are machine-knitted on a manually operated machine, then sewn and finished by hand.
