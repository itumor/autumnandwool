## 1. /how-to-wash-a-wool-sweater

**Current H1:** How to Wash a Wool Sweater at Home
**Current opening (summary):** Hand wash in cool water with a mild wool detergent, avoid wringing, dry flat, and store folded.

**New opening (50 words):**
> Hand wash a wool sweater in cool water with a mild wool detergent. Press it gently, never wring or twist it, and dry it flat on a towel away from heat. Store it folded, not hung. Check the care line on your product page first.

**Before / after:** Before had no named author and no "check the product page" in the first lines. After: byline added, same steps, quotable in one block, care-line check moved to the end of the answer.

**Question H2s (replace current H2s):**

1. **Can you wash a wool sweater at home?**
   Yes, by hand. Check the care line on the product page first. Use cool water and a mild wool detergent. Hot water and heat plus rubbing can cause felting.
2. **How do you hand wash a wool sweater?**
   Press the sweater gently in the water with slow, light movements. Lift it out with both hands, supporting its full weight. Do not wring or twist it.
3. **How do you dry a wool sweater?**
   Lay it flat on a clean towel and roll gently to remove some water. Reshape by hand. Keep it away from radiators and direct heat. Do not tumble dry.
4. **How should you store a wool sweater?**
   Fold it. Do not hang it, as hanging can stretch the shoulders. The FAQ suggests breathable bags with cedar or lavender to deter moths.
5. **What should you avoid?**
   Hot water, bleach and strong detergents. Rubbing the knit. Ironing over the knit. Hanging it wet or drying it in direct sun.

**Before / after:** Before opened with a definition and held the answer lower down. After: the choice and the warmth point sit in the first 3 sentences. Same facts as the current page.

**Question H2s:**

1. **What are merino and lambswool?**
   Merino comes from merino sheep and is generally finer. Lambswool comes from young sheep, usually at the first shearing.
2. **Which feels softer, merino or lambswool?**
   Merino feels smooth against the skin. Lambswool feels softer and springier. Feel is personal, so try the one that matches how you like a knit to wear.
3. **Is merino or lambswool warmer?**
   Neither is automatically warmer. Yarn weight and knit decide warmth.
4. **How do you care for merino and lambswool?**
   Both: hand wash in cool water with mild wool detergent, do not wring, dry flat. Full steps in [How to wash a wool sweater](https://www.autumnandwool.com/how-to-wash-a-wool-sweater).
5. **Which should you choose?**
   Merino for a finer, smoother feel and next-to-skin layers. Lambswool for a softer, springier knit.

**New opening (55 words):**
> Choose your size from your chest, waist and hip measurements, then check the garment width on the product page. Double the flat width to get the full circumference and compare it with your chest. The difference is your ease, the extra room. Vests and many oversized pieces come in one size.

**Before / after:** Before explained what oversized means. After: the method (measure, double, compare) is the answer, and the one-size warning is in the opening. Same facts.

**Question H2s:**

1. **What do relaxed and oversized mean?**
   The size guide lists Close-fitting, Relaxed, Oversized and One size. Each product page has its own fit note, for example "Oversized; roomy and easy to layer."
2. **How do you measure for an oversized sweater?**
   Measure chest, waist and hips over thin, fitted clothing, tape snug but comfortable. Table below.
3. **How do you compare garment measurements?**
   Double the flat width to get the circumference, then compare with your chest. Example: Distressed Oversized Wool Raglan Sweater is 57 cm wide flat; Oversized Merino Turtleneck in Dusty Pink is 53 cm wide flat.
4. **Which pieces come in one size?**
   Vests, some cardigans and many oversized pieces. The Merino Wool Raglan Cardigan is one size, listed width 61 cm. Check sizes on each product page.
5. **Does layering change the fit?**
   Yes. A vest over a shirt fits differently from a loose knit over a fitted top.
