How our knitwear is made

Every Autumn & Wool piece is designed by Kristine in Riga, Latvia. Our About page says every garment is made in Latvia from merino, lambswool or pure new wool. This page explains what that means in practice, in plain terms.

Designed in Riga

Kristine designs every piece, inspired by a family tradition of knitting. Our studio is in Riga, Latvia, and it is a small one. The exact fibre for each style is listed on its product page, so you can check it before you buy.

Knitted on a manual machine

Our knitwear is knitted on a manual knitting machine. Nothing here comes off a factory line, as our About page puts it. The machine is operated by hand, and the knitting is done in our Riga studio.

Finished by hand

After knitting, each piece is finished by hand. The FAQ lists the finishing steps as hand-sewing, finishing, washing and pressing before the piece ships.

How we describe the process

Because the knitting is done on a machine, we describe our process as machine knitting with hand finishing. This is the wording we use to describe our process.

Why it matters when you buy

Knowing how a piece is made helps you read its product page. The fibre, fit and care steps are listed on each page, and our size guide explains how to choose a size. See our wool sweaters for the fibres in use.
