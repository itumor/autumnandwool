## Rules
1. One pin per zone, linked to the gift page (`/shop/winter-wool-gifts`, gift guide proposal option A), not to a PDP:
   a gift page can swap products as the GREEN list changes, a pin can't.
2. Dates appear **only** in the pin description and the optional overlay, copied exactly from the approved OPS-09
   row. Published date = what OPS publishes (ruling C3), no rounding, no "guaranteed".
3. Products in the pin image: only SKUs on that week's GREEN list (G2), in a colour with a real photo (ruling C7).
4. Pinterest pins live for months. The owner edits each pin the day after its cut-off: switch the text to the gift
   card fallback (row GC below) or archive it. Calendar reminders are listed in the last table.
5. US / Canada / Australia: no date pin. Per ruling C3 the message is "Christmas delivery not guaranteed", so the
   only pin for those zones is the digital gift card.
6. Calm tone (aw-brand-voice): no countdowns, no "hurry", no "last chance", no exclamation marks.
