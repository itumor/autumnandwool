Wool cardigans for every day

Our wool cardigans come in three shapes: a long open-front cardigan with a shawl collar, a straight open-front style with ribbed side slits, and a merino raglan cardigan in black, brown or blue. The shawl-collar and side-slit cardigans are 100% pure new wool, and the raglan is 100% merino wool, as stated on each product page.
