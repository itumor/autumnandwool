# Autumn & Wool short video scripts

Purpose: 3-5 short vertical videos a week that show real garments and real making, and send qualified visits to
the store (README s4). Measure by visits and orders from UTM links, not views.

Read first: `.claude/skills/aw-brand-voice/SKILL.md` (claim rules apply to voice-over, on-screen text, captions).

## Formats that fit this brand (pick one per script)

| Format | Length | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Making-of | 15-30 s | Yarn cone -> machine -> hand-sewing seams -> finished piece folded. Result first or last |
| Texture close-up | 7-15 s | Macro of rib, cuff, drape; hand stretches and releases; one fact on screen |
| Fit / try-on | 15-30 s | Real person, front-side-back, sits, raises arms; size + height on screen |
| Styling 3 ways | 20-40 s | One piece, three everyday outfits; garment colour stays true |
| Founder voice | 20-45 s | Kristine talks to camera or over hands working; one memory or one process fact |
| Answer a question | 15-30 s | Real customer/FAQ question on screen, show the answer (sizing, care, delivery) |
| Gift / season | 10-20 s | Hat & scarf set or beanie wrapped, care card, parcel leaving the studio |

## Procedure

1. Pick the SKU from Production's safe-to-promote list. Pull its live facts: name, colours, fibre wording,
   price, measurements, model size. Missing fact -> `[VERIFY]`.
2. Pick one format and one message. One video = one idea.
3. Write 3 hook options. Hook = what is SEEN in second 0-1 + on-screen text + first spoken line.
   TikTok's own guidance: proposition in the first 3 s, hook within 6 s. Aim for movement in frame at 0 s.
   Hook types that suit us: process reveal ("This sweater starts as one cone of yarn"), detail
   ("Watch the rib spring back"), honest answer ("Does merino itch? Here is how this one feels"; answer only with
   true product facts), founder ("15 years ago I started on Etsy as Mareshop").
4. Write the beat sheet (template below). Shots must be filmable by one person with a phone in the Riga studio.
5. On-screen text: max 2 lines, 3-6 words a line, inside the centre safe zone (keep clear of the bottom
   ~20% and right edge UI). Videos are mostly watched muted: the story must work with text alone.
6. Caption + CTA + UTM link (template). Max 3 hashtags.
7. Run the aw-brand-voice checklist. Save to `campaign-assets/drafts/video-scripts-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

## Weekly batch (default)

- 1 making-of, 1 texture or fit, 1 styling or question, + 1-2 optional (founder, gift).
- Reuse: each Reel gets a TikTok version (same footage, platform text), 1-2 stills for Pinterest/Stories.
- Film in one 2-hour session where possible; list all shots across scripts in one shot list.

## Guardrails

- Real footage of the real garment. No AI-generated garments, models or "making" scenes presented as real.
  AI may help with ideas, cut lists, subtitles, noise removal; never change colour, shape, texture or fit.
- Colour grading must keep the garment's true colour (compare against the product in daylight).
- No invented customer quotes, no fake "POV: customer" skits implying real buyers.
- People on camera other than Kristine: written consent first. No children.
- No trending audio you cannot license for business use; use the platform's commercial library.
- Never post, upload or schedule. Drafts only, owner approves.
