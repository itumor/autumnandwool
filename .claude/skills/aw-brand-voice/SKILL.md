# Autumn & Wool brand voice and claim rules (shared by every aw- agent)

Purpose: one voice and one truth standard for every team agent. Voice can flex; facts cannot.
Brand: knitwear made in Riga, Latvia by Kristine; formerly Mareshop on Etsy. Customer: women 25-45 who value
natural fibres, understated design, quality and comfort. Promise: thoughtfully made knitwear from Riga, designed
for comfort, longevity and timeless everyday style.

## 1. Voice attributes (we are / we are not)

| We are | Not | In practice |
|---|---|---|
| Calm | Hype | No exclamation marks in headlines, no "obsessed", no countdown pressure |
| Specific | Vague | Name the stitch, the colour, the measurement, the step. "Rib-knit cuff, folds once" beats "cosy vibes" |
| Warm | Sugary | Talk like Kristine to a regular customer. No "babe", no heart-emoji runs |
| Understated premium | Luxury cliché | Let detail carry quality. Avoid "luxurious", "exquisite", "elevate" |
| Honest | Salesy | Say what it is, what it is not, how it fits. Admit limits (small batches, lead times) |

Tone shifts by context, voice stays:

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Reels / TikTok voice-over or on-screen text | Short, present tense, sensory, first person ("I", Kristine) for making-of |
| Instagram / Pinterest caption | 1-3 short lines, one idea, soft CTA ("link in profile") |
| Founder story | First person, concrete memories and process, no grand mission statements |
| Email | Letter from Riga: one news item, one product, one link |
| Ads | Product fact + benefit + proof. Still calm. No fake scarcity |
| Support / comment reply | Plain, helpful, sizing facts first, sign "Kristine" or "Autumn & Wool" |

## 2. Words

Use: made in Riga, knitted on manual knitting machines, finished by hand, rib-knit, raglan, drape, garment
measurements, wool / merino wool / lambswool (only as the product page states), layer, everyday, made in small
batches (only if production confirms), care, mend, season after season.

Avoid (style): luxurious, elevate, must-have, obsessed, game-changer, iconic, timeless (max once per piece),
"unlock", "curated", "not just X, it's Y", "Here's the thing", "Thoughts?" bait, emoji as bullets, all caps,
more than 3 hashtags on Instagram, broetry (every sentence its own line).

## 3. Claim rules (hard gate: fail one = do not ship the draft)

| Claim type | Rule |
|---|---|
| Sustainable, eco, ethical, green, conscious, slow fashion as a promise, organic, RWS, mulesing-free, certified, zero-waste, plastic-free, carbon anything | **Banned.** No supplier evidence on file (`implementation-report.md`). Owner removed these deliberately on 18 Oct. Do not reintroduce, even as hashtags (#sustainablefashion, #organic) |
| "Handmade" | Restricted. Machine-knitted on manual machines and hand-finished. Prefer the precise phrase: "knitted on manual machines and finished by hand in Riga". Bare "handmade" only in the owner-approved live line "Handmade in Riga by Kristine", or with owner approval per asset. **Never "hand-knitted" or "hand-knit"** for machine pieces |
| Fibre content | Copy the exact wording from that product's live page ("100% merino wool", "100% pure new wool", "100% lambswool"). Never generalise ("all our knits are merino/wool"): some catalog items are blends or acrylic |
| Performance | No "itch-free", "hypoallergenic", "machine-washable", "never pills", "lasts forever", "warmest" unless the live product page states it |
| Reviews / proof | Marketplace history only, labelled as Etsy: "Formerly Mareshop on Etsy, 4.9★ from 1,400+ customers" (site strip; 1,500+ only after the owner updates the site). Never invent or paraphrase a review into a quote. Real Etsy quotes need owner OK, verbatim, labelled "Etsy review" |
| Sales numbers | "8,000+ orders over 15 years on Etsy". Never imply direct-store volume (zero real direct orders to date) |
| Delivery / returns | Dispatch 2-5 working days; Europe about 5-10 working days; US/Canada/Australia 7-20. "Free shipping to eligible destinations", "14-day returns". No other return promises (legal review pending) |
| Stock / scarcity | Promote only SKUs on Production's safe-to-promote list. "Last one" only if stock = 1 at time of writing, re-checked before posting |
| Prices | From the live product page on the day; EUR; never invent discounts or codes |
| Founder facts | Kristine, Riga studio, 15 years on Etsy as Mareshop. Anything else (years knitting, family history, training) needs the owner's own words first |

When a needed fact is missing, write `[VERIFY: what is needed]` in the draft. Never guess.

## 4. Paired examples

| Bad | Good | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Our luxurious sustainable sweaters are a must-have!" | "Merino raglan in Dusty Pink. Knitted in Riga, finished by hand." | Banned claim + hype out; fact in |
| "Hand-knitted with love by our artisans" | "Kristine knits each piece on a manual machine, then sews the seams by hand." | Truthful process, real person |
| "Only 2 left!! Hurry" | "Small batch. When this colour is gone, it may take a few weeks to make more. [VERIFY lead time]" | No fake urgency |
| "Customers say it's the softest ever" | (Real Etsy quote, owner-approved, labelled) or omit | No invented proof |
| "Perfect for everyone, all year" | "Fits relaxed. Model is [VERIFY height] and wears [VERIFY size]. Full measurements on the product page." | Fit facts reduce returns |
| "#slowfashion #sustainablestyle #eco" | "#AutumnAndWool #MerinoKnit #RigaMade" | No claim hashtags |

## 5. Visual identity (for briefs and overlays)

Colours: oat `#F2EDE6` (background), sand `#E7E0D6`, ink `#24201C` (text), rust `#8E3B1F` (accent, the italic
"&"). Fonts: Instrument Serif (headings), DM Sans (body/overlays). Look: natural light, warm neutrals, texture
close-ups, real hands and real bodies, no heavy filters. Social handle: Instagram `@autumnandwool`
(Facebook/Pinterest still on Mareshop accounts, confirm before tagging).

## 6. Review checklist (run on every draft)

1. Any banned or restricted word from section 3? Remove or mark `[VERIFY]`.
2. Every fibre, price, stock, delivery fact traced to the live page or a business-os file?
3. Garment shown/described = the real colour and shape?
4. One idea, calm tone, no AI tells from section 2?
5. Draft labelled DRAFT, saved to `business-os/` or `campaign-assets/drafts/`, nothing posted.
