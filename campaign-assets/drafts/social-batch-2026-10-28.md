# Autumn & Wool: first social content batch

DRAFT — for owner review. Prepared 28 October 2026. Proposed dates are editorial slots, not scheduled posts. Captions below are final draft wording; factual and asset checks are kept beside them so they cannot be mistaken for approved posts.

## Direction

Show the work behind the knitwear: the machine, the hands, the finishing. Use one clear detail per post, calm wording and real footage. Start with Instagram. Pinterest adaptations are banked until its account is confirmed. TikTok is deferred because the repository has conflicting account assumptions.

The existing W44/W45 plans disagree about accounts and product eligibility. The wave-3 GREEN list is explicitly assumed, and the reviewed checkout has no W44 capacity file. Treat no SKU as confirmed for promotion. The first calendar therefore uses making and studio story only. No price, fibre percentage, delivery promise, sales number or testimonial is needed in this batch.

Audience: people considering everyday knitwear who want to understand how it is made. Proposed posting window: 18:00–20:00 Europe/Riga, an initial experiment rather than an established best time. Owner: social manager prepares drafts and edits; Kristine/owner supplies and confirms footage, reviews factual wording and publishes approved pieces.
