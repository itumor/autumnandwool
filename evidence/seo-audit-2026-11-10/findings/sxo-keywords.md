# SXO and keyword fit: autumnandwool.com (2026-11-10)

Scope: 12 target queries, SERP page-type check, mismatch findings, cannibalisation clusters, content list.
Method: WebSearch (standard mode, US index, one run per query, 2026-11-10). Result order = order returned by the tool, not verified Google positions. Page-type counts are approximate, from the link lists returned (about 15-20 links per query). On-site facts come from `crawl/pages.tsv` and `crawl/html/*.html` (static HTML, not rendered).
Constraint: Squarespace 7.1, no code injection. Product data is frozen by the owner, so all product-level changes are proposals.
Layer key: (a) page/product SEO settings, (b) 301 URL mapping, (d) content edit or new page (Squarespace blog or regular page), (f) not fixable on this plan.

Persona check (first-time buyer):
- Homepage: clear what is sold (sweaters, long cardigans, accessories) and by whom (Kristine, Riga, Latvia). Prices in EUR visible (e.g. long cardigan EUR 145). Delivery and returns in the trust strip ("Free shipping", "14-day returns"). Gap: no UK wording; the "Formerly Mareshop" link is the only trust history. Review claim "4.9 from 1,400+ customers" is on-site copy, not verified here.
- Product page (grey lambswool): price EUR 136, sizes S-XL, size conversions EU/US/UK, measurements per size, care text, "Free shipping, in-stock ship from Riga within 2-5 working days, 14-day returns". Gap: "Hand" vs "machine" wording (X-04); no fit or "how it wears" line beyond "Relaxed"; no fibre-plus-care summary above the fold (not checked in rendered view).
- Overall: trust and sizing are good. Gaps are destination clarity (UK), the hand vs machine claim, and the lack of guide content for research-stage buyers.
