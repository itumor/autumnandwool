# Content Quality and On-Page SEO: autumnandwool.com (2026-11-10)

Scope: 40 HTML pages in the crawl (39 indexable; /cart is noindex). Data: `crawl/pages.tsv` and `crawl/html/*.html`, no live fetch. All copy below is a proposal. Product data is frozen by the owner.

Platform layers: (a) Squarespace page/product SEO settings, (d) content edit in the editor or product description, (f) not fixable on this plan (no code injection or scripts).

## Correction to the crawl table

`pages.tsv` shows meta descriptions of "Explore Autumn & Wool" (21 chars) on /privacy-policy and "Lightweight women" (17 chars) on the lightweight turtleneck PDP. The raw HTML does not match. The crawl cut the text at the apostrophe. Raw lengths are:

- /privacy-policy: 158 chars. This is slightly too long, not too short.
- Lightweight turtleneck PDP: 130 chars. This is in range, but the wording is weak ("Lightweight women's").

All other titles and meta descriptions in `pages.tsv` match the raw HTML (checked for all 40 pages).

## What works

- Every product page has a unique title and meta description, and each one names the colour.
- Self-referencing canonicals on all 22 product pages and on the info pages.
- Product JSON-LD on 22 product pages (`Product`, `AggregateOffer`, `Offer`). Organisation and LocalBusiness on every page.
- Named maker: "Kristine designs each piece in Riga" (FAQ, about). Company name, SIA registration number and Riga address on /contact and /imprint. Imprint has "Last updated: 1 November 2026".
- Size guide has a body chart in cm and inch, how-to-measure steps and garment measurement guidance (352 words).
- Product pages give the exact fibre (for example "Material: 100% pure new wool") and care instructions. 19 of 22 product descriptions include a garment measurement block.
- FAQ has 14 questions across 6 topics. Answers are quotable and concrete (for example "In-stock items leave Riga, Latvia within 2-5 working days").
- robots.txt does not block AI crawlers (named bots sit in the `*` group, which disallows only internal paths such as /config, /search, /api/ and /static/). llms.txt is present.
- /cart is noindex. Sitemap lists 22 product pages and 17 other pages.

## Not checked

- No live fetch. All data comes from the saved crawl (`crawl/pages.tsv`, `crawl/html`).
- Squarespace plan features were not verified: blog availability, URL mappings, related-products block, category canonical settings, H1 rendering in the template. Layer labels that say "a or f" or "d or f" depend on these.
- No Search Console, analytics or rank data was used.
- Readability: average sentence length only, no Flesch or Gunning score.
- Image alt: only the count of tags with no alt. Alt quality was not reviewed.
- The QRG scorer returned 93-96 on every page tested, so it was not used for the scores.
- Word counts from `pages.tsv` include navigation. Main-text counts in this report come from the `<main>` element, and product description counts come from the description block, which the DOM repeats twice.
- Prices, stock, delivery claims and Etsy history were not verified.
- The "Old product URL 404" finding (O-18) was not checked for external links.
