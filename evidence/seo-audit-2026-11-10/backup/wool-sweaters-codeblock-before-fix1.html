Wool sweaters for layering and everyday wear

Most of our wool sweaters are relaxed or oversized, cut for layering over a shirt or under a coat. The fibre is merino wool, lambswool or pure new wool, and the exact fibre is stated on each product page. The range covers raglan sweaters, stand-collar and side-slit styles, and a lightweight merino turtleneck that sits close to the body. Oversized shapes are roomy by design, so compare the garment measurements with your own before you choose a size. Our size guide shows how to measure your chest, waist and hips, with a chart for sizes S to XL and notes on garment measurements. Each piece is designed by Kristine in Riga, Latvia, and knitted on a manual knitting machine and finished by hand.
