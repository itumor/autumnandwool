Orders & payment

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards through our secure checkout. You will receive an order confirmation by email.

How long does it take to process my order?

In-stock items leave Riga, Latvia within 2–5 working days. Made-to-order items ship within the time shown on the product page.

Shipping & taxes

What are your shipping times?

Europe: about 5–10 working days. USA, Canada and Australia: 7–20 days. Other destinations are shown at checkout. These are estimates, not guarantees. Shipping is free to Latvia, the US and other destinations offered at checkout.

Do I have to pay customs or taxes?

For EU customers VAT is included in the price. Outside the EU, customs or import duties may apply depending on local rules.

Returns & exchanges

What is your return policy?

Returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery for unworn, unwashed items in original condition. Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item is faulty. See Shipping & returns for full terms.

Do you offer exchanges?

Yes. Place a new order for the size or colour you prefer and return the original. We refund the first purchase once the return arrives.

Materials & care

What materials do you use?

Merino wool, lambswool or pure new wool. The exact fibre is listed on each product page.

How are your pieces made?

Kristine designs each piece in Riga. It is knitted on a manual knitting machine in Latvia, then hand-sewn, finished, washed and pressed before shipping.

How should I care for my knitwear?

Hand wash in cold water with a wool-safe detergent. Do not tumble dry — lay flat to dry. Store folded, not hung, in breathable bags with cedar or lavender to keep moths away.

Sizing

How do I choose the right size?

Each product page lists garment measurements, and our size guide explains how to compare them with your body. Need help? Email autumnandwool@gmail.com.

Do you offer custom sizing?

Not at the moment. Many of our pieces have relaxed silhouettes designed to suit a range of body types.
