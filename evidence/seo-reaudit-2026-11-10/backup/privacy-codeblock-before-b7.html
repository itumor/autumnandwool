At Autumn & Wool, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit www.autumnandwool.com or contact us directly.

1. Who we are

Autumn & Wool is a handmade knitwear brand based in Riga, Latvia, creating sustainable garments from 100% natural yarns.

If you have questions about this policy or your data, please contact us at autumnandwool@gmail.com.

2. What information we collect

We may collect:

  • Personal details (name, email, address, phone number) — when you place an order or contact us.
  • Payment details — processed securely by third-party providers (Stripe, through our secure Squarespace checkout); we never store card data.
  • Website usage data — through cookies and analytics tools (e.g. Squarespace Analytics, Google Analytics) to improve website performance.

3. How we use your information

Your data helps us to:

  • Process and deliver your orders.
  • Communicate about your purchase, delivery, or support requests.
  • Send updates, offers, or newsletters (only with your consent).
  • Improve our website and customer experience.

We never sell or rent your data to third parties.

4. Cookies

Our site uses cookies to ensure a smooth browsing experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some site features may not work correctly.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your personal data only as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations (e.g. tax and accounting laws).

6. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Request data portability.

To exercise these rights, contact autumnandwool@gmail.com.

7. Third-party services

We may use trusted third-party providers to operate our store and analytics (e.g. Squarespace, Stripe, Etsy, Instagram). Each provider has its own privacy policy governing data use.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with the revised date.

Questions about your data?

Write to autumnandwool@gmail.com · Autumn & Wool, Riga, Latvia
