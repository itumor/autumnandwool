Oversized fit guide

Oversized and relaxed knits fit differently from close-fitting knitwear. They are cut wider and longer, and they sit away from the body. This guide explains how to choose a size and how to read the measurements on our product pages.

Relaxed and oversized: what the words mean

Our size guide lists two fit types, Relaxed and Oversized. Oversized pieces are roomy and made for layering. Each product page gives its own fit note, such as 'Relaxed' or 'Oversized; roomy and easy to layer'. Some pieces, such as the lightweight merino turtleneck, are described as close to the body. Check which fit each style uses before you choose.

Measure your body first

Measure over thin, fitted clothing, with the tape snug but comfortable. Our size guide lists body measurements for each size. For example, size S is 84 to 90 cm around the chest, 62 to 70 cm at the waist and 90 to 96 cm at the hips. Size M is 90 to 96 cm at the chest. Sizes S to XL convert to EU 36 to 42, UK 8 to 14 and US 6 to 12.

Check the garment measurements

Body measurements are only part of the picture. Oversized pieces are cut wider than the body, so compare the garment measurements on the product page, for example the Distressed Oversized Wool Raglan Sweater, with your own chest. The size guide gives an example: a Pure Wool Side-Slit Sweater in size S measures 50 cm flat, which is about 100 cm (39.4 in) around.

One-size pieces

Vests, some cardigans and oversized pieces are often one size. The size guide advises that you compare the garment width with your chest. Other pieces list sizes from S to XL, or S/M and L/XL, so check the sizes on the product page before you order. Our sweater vests are often one size.

Layering

Oversized knits are made for layering, so think about what goes underneath. A sweater vest over a shirt gives a different shape from a loose knit over a fitted top. Our beige melange vest is described as easy to layer over shirts and dresses.

Still unsure?

If you are between sizes, read the product page first and then send us a question through our contact page. We reply within 1 to 2 business days.
