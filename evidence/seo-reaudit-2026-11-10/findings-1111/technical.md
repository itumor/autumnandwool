# Technical SEO findings: autumnandwool.com re-audit 2026-11-11

Scope: 49 URLs from the on-disk crawl (`crawl-1111/index.tsv`), sitemap.xml, robots.txt, llms.txt, probes.txt. Live requests used: 4 of 6 (`/cart` GET, `/home` GET, `/shop/gift` GET, `/cart` headers and body). Judged on observed source only.

Weighting: items tagged **platform** are generated by Squarespace and the owner cannot edit them. They are scored at reduced weight. Items tagged **fixable** are owner-editable and count at full weight.

## Technical score: 72 / 100

Crawl and indexability are clean: all 49 URLs return 200 with no redirect chain, no noindex, content is server-rendered, and the sitemap covers every crawled page. The deductions come mainly from platform-generated canonical, sitemap and H1 gaps (reduced weight), plus three owner-fixable items: the missing H1 on the Cookie Policy page, Etsy-style product URL slugs, and heavy image payloads on `/shop`.

## Verified OK

- All 49 crawled URLs: HTTP 200, no redirect chain on crawled URLs.
- `http://autumnandwool.com/`, `https://autumnandwool.com/`, `http://www.autumnandwool.com/` each 301 to `https://www.autumnandwool.com/` (probes.txt).
- Unknown path `/this-is-404-probe` returns 404.
- Old product URL `/shop/p/hand-knitted-lambswool-...` returns a single 301 to `/shop/p/p/...`.
- HTTPS everywhere; HSTS present on all 49 pages.
- No noindex on any of the 49 pages (meta robots and X-Robots-Tag both absent).
- `/cart` returns 200 with `<meta name="ROBOTS" content="NOINDEX">` (live). Correct behaviour.
- Self-canonical on 41 of 49 pages (root canonical is `https://www.autumnandwool.com`, matching `/`).
- Title present on all 49 pages. Meta description present on 42 of 49.
- Exactly one H1 on 40 of 49 pages; no multiple H1s.
- Viewport `width=device-width, initial-scale=1` on all 49 pages.
- Content is server-rendered: 190–950 words of body text in raw HTML on every page, no SPA shell, product names and EUR prices in raw HTML.
- Product JSON-LD on 22 product pages: Product plus Offer, EUR price, InStock/SoldOut availability.
- Organization, LocalBusiness and WebSite JSON-LD on all pages.
- No active mixed content (no `src="http://"` or `href="http://"` on scripts, images, stylesheets).
- Internal link audit: no broken internal hrefs in static HTML. The only non-crawled target is `/cart` (200, noindex).
- Alt attribute present on all images checked (0 `<img>` without alt on home, shop, product, about).
- Sitemap: 48 URLs, all `https://www.autumnandwool.com`, every one resolves to a crawled 200. Every crawled page except `/` appears in the sitemap.
- robots.txt: valid, declares `Sitemap: https://www.autumnandwool.com/sitemap.xml`. AI crawler user-agents (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, etc.) are listed in the same group as `*` and get only the narrow platform disallows (`/config`, `/search`, `/api/`, query-param variants). They are not blocked site-wide.
- llms.txt: 41 URLs, all resolve to 200 in the crawl set. None missing.
- No hreflang present (not applicable to this single-locale site; no hreflang validation was needed).
